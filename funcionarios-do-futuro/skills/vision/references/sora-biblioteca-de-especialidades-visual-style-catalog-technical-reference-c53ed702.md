# SORA BIBLIOTECA DE ESPECIALIDADES – Visual Style Catalog & Technical Reference

## Descrição executiva

This critical knowledge base serves as the comprehensive visual style catalog for SORA Prompt Architect, containing curated collections of editorial, cinematographic, and artistic aesthetics. It provides standardized texture libraries, lighting configurations, and camera/lens specifications that ensure consistent high-quality output. This foundation enables rapid style identification, technical parameter selection, and maintains professional standards across all prompt generation and reverse-engineering tasks. Essential for achieving photorealistic and luxury editorial results.

## Catálogo de Estilos Visuais

### Editorial Fashion
- High-contrast lighting with soft shadows
- Clean backgrounds with minimal distractions
- Sharp focus on subject with controlled depth of field
- Color palettes: monochromatic, earth tones, or bold accent colors
- Preferred lenses: 85mm, 135mm portrait lenses

### Cinematographic Styles
- Film noir: Hard key lighting, dramatic shadows, high contrast
- Golden hour: Warm color temperature, soft directional light
- Cyberpunk: Neon lighting, cool color palette, urban environments
- Documentary: Natural lighting, handheld camera movement
- Commercial: Even lighting, saturated colors, polished aesthetics

### Luxury Editorial
- Soft box lighting with fill cards
- Premium material textures (silk, cashmere, leather)
- Minimal color palettes with metallic accents
- Architectural backgrounds or natural environments
- Medium format camera aesthetics

### Camera/Lens Preferences
- **Cinema Prime 35mm**: Wide environmental context, minimal distortion
- **85mm Portrait**: Natural perspective, beautiful bokeh characteristics
- **Anamorphic 50mm**: Horizontal flares, cinematic aspect ratio
- **Macro 100mm**: Extreme detail capture, texture emphasis
- **Wide 24mm**: Environmental storytelling, architectural integration

## Boas práticas & pitfalls

### Best Practices
- Always specify exact camera and lens combinations for consistency
- Include texture details for photorealistic results
- Balance technical precision with creative storytelling
- Use professional lighting terminology for accurate interpretation
- Maintain color temperature consistency throughout prompt

### Common Pitfalls to Avoid
- Mixing contradictory lighting setups (hard and soft simultaneously)
- Overloading prompts with too many visual effects
- Using vague descriptors like "beautiful" or "stunning"
- Ignoring physical lighting laws and camera limitations
- Combining incompatible aesthetic styles without purpose
